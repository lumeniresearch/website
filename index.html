LUMEHARNESS

The engine behind Lumeni

Markdown notes Chat history Papers liked & passed on

LumeHarness is the infrastructure that filters through everything Lumeni remembers about you as a researcher — your markdown notes, your full chat history, and the papers you've liked or passed on — and decides what's actually worth pulling into context. Instead of re-feeding Lumeni everything you've ever discussed, LumeHarness surfaces only what's relevant to the idea in front of you right now.

That's how it can save so many tokens.
